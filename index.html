Storage & Product Information

How to keep your juice fresh

🌡️ Not refrigerated: consume within a day.

❄️ Refrigerated at 4–6 °C: consume within 3 days.

🧊 Frozen at −18 °C: keeps for up to 1 month.

Always follow the storage instructions and use-by date printed on your bottle.
